# Module Walkthrough Checklist

Use this for a quick pass before writing the full review.

## A. Module Basics
- What is the module name?
- Who uses it?
- At what step of the office workflow is it used?
- What should happen before and after it?

## B. Business Fit
- Does it reflect real gyoseishoshi operations?
- Are any essential steps missing?
- Does it mix client data and matter data incorrectly?
- Would users avoid it and use another tool?

## C. Form And Fields
- Are the required fields truly required at this stage?
- Is anything duplicated?
- Is field order natural?
- Are labels clear?
- Is structured data being buried in notes?

## D. Usability
- Can a new staff member understand it?
- Can an experienced staff member complete it quickly?
- Are there too many clicks?
- Is there any likely omission or error point?

## E. Efficiency
- Does it reduce manual entry?
- Does it reduce searching and back-and-forth?
- Does it reduce internal confirmations?
- Does it help standardization and handoff?

## F. Risks
- What will likely break in real use?
- What will staff skip?
- What will cause future rework?

## G. Decision
- Pass
- Optimize
- Redesign
